PFS apps on your phone

Put an app on your home screen with the PFS logo. Tap it and the app opens straight away.

PFS HQEvery app you have, in one place

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap the app you want above.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap the app you want above.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.

The first time, the app asks you to sign in with your Premier Google account. You only see the apps PFS HQ gives you. Google shows a thin bar above its app pages; that's normal.
